## 🎬 Storyboard & Scene Breakdown

* **Visual:** Close-up of an exhausted founder switching between 25 open browser tabs, broken Slack notifications, and a messy spreadsheet titled "Launch Checklist". Dark, frantic lighting.
* **On-Screen Text:** "Still managing your tech stack in 15 different tabs?"
* **Voiceover (HeyGen Avatar / Presenter in Corner Bubble):**
  *"If you're still spending 15 hours a week duct-taping APIs, chasing site audits, and writing launch copy by hand... stop."*

### Scene 2: The Solution Intro (0:10–0:22)
* **Visual:** High-contrast transition to a sleek, dark-mode UI with illuminated swarm nodes connecting in real-time. Clean, luminous UI with gentle motion.
* **On-Screen Text:** "Meet Buzz Swarm OS: Your Autonomous AI Department."
* **Voiceover:**
  *"Meet Buzz Swarm OS. One lightweight command center that deploys an entire autonomous department of AI specialists in under 60 seconds."*

### Scene 3: Workflow Step 1 — Instant Command Trigger (0:22–0:40)
* **Visual:** Dynamic zoom-in on the terminal/chat window. User types `./manage.sh send marketing "@marketer-head Launch Q3 campaign"`.
* **Visual Callout:** Kinetic spotlight badge: *1. Single Command Dispatch*.
* **Voiceover:**

### Scene 4: Workflow Step 2 — Autonomous Execution & Verification (0:40–0:60)
* **Visual:** Split screen showing live real-time output: copywriter generating graded sales copy on the left, DevOps running server health checks on the right.
* **Visual Callout:** Kinetic badge: *2. Parallel Autonomous Workflows*.
* **Voiceover:**

* **Visual Callout:** Kinetic badge: *3. Cryptographically Verified Results*.

* **Visual:** Clean metric counter animating from "15 Hours/Week" down to "3 Minutes". High-energy avatar presenter full frame or side-by-side.
