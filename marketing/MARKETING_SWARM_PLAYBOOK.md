## 🎯 How the Marketing Swarm Uses This Library

### 1. `@marketer-growth` (Campaigns & Funnels)
* Constructs **Grand Slam Offers** and **Godfather Offers** using Hormozi's and Suby's value equations.
* Designs outbound email sequences with 5-phase escalation and 9-word re-engagement triggers.
* Implements direct WhatsApp conversion scripts and broadcast flows.

### 2. `@marketer-research` (Market Intelligence & Lead Scoring)
* Uses Suby's **Halo Strategy** and Akin Alabi's **Market Demand Filters** to find high-intent prospects in Nigeria, Africa, and global markets.
* Scrapes company data via Firecrawl and scores leads based on budget, urgency, and technical need.

### 3. `@marketer-content` (Copywriting & Proposals)
* Writes proposals and SOWs grounded in **Paul Smith's Storytelling Framework** and **Dan Lok's High-Ticket Framing**.
* Crafts cold outreach, landing page headlines, video scripts (HeyGen/Veo), and LinkedIn thought-leadership posts.

### 4. `@ceo` (Executive Deals & Negotiation)
* Uses **Brian Tracy's Negotiation Principles** and **John Maxwell's Laws of Leadership** when closing high-value corporate and government contracts.
