## 🤖 The Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Standard

AI search engines index websites differently from traditional crawlers. They favor structured markdown summaries, explicit entity relationships, and fast semantic parsing.

### The `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` Standard
1. **Location**: Hosted at the root of the domain (`/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt`).
2. **Format**: Clean, structured Markdown providing:
   - Official company identity, vision, and core capabilities.
   - Authoritative descriptions of flagship software products.
   - Verified pricing models and contact coordinates.
   - Direct documentation and API specification links.
