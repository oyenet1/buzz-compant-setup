# 🔺 Media Buyer & Kill Calls Playbook
**Agent Handle:** `@marketer-media-buyer` (Aliases: `@media-buyer`)

---

## 🎯 Role Overview
The **Media Buyer** manages paid traffic distribution across Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), Google Search & YouTube, TikTok, and X/Twitter. This role is governed by mathematical discipline: enforcing ruthless **Kill Calls**, diagnosing **Ad Fatigue vs. Dead Audiences**, and scaling winning creative angles.

---

## 🛑 Diagnosing Fatigue vs Dead Audiences

| Diagnostic Metric | Ad Fatigue Signal | Dead Audience Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Frequency (7-day)** | High (>3.5) | Low to Medium (1.1–2.0) |
| **Click-Through Rate (CTR Link)** | Declining (>30% drop from Day 1) | Consistently Low (<0.8%) from start |
| **Cost Per Click (CPC)** | Rising steadily | High from Day 1 |
| **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)** | Spiked after 14+ days of profitability | Never achieved target CPA |
| **Remedy** | **Refresh Creative**: Swap visual hook or intro while keeping audience. | **Kill Audience**: Shift budget to broader or lookalike audience. |

## 💰 Budget Pacing Rules
1. **Testing Phase (ABO)**: $20–$50/day per ad set with 3–5 creative variants.
2. **Scaling Phase (CBO / Advantage+)**: Consolidate top 3 winning creatives into broad targeting with budget increases capped at 20% every 48 hours to prevent machine learning reset.
