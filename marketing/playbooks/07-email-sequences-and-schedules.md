## 📅 The 5-Day Soap Opera Sequence (Welcome & Nurture)

| Day | Send Time | Subject Line Strategy | Core Psychological Angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Immediate (T+0m) | "Here is your access (+ quick question)" | Instant value delivery, set open expectations, open loop for Day 2. |
| **Day 2** | T+24h (09:00 AM) | "The $10,000 automation mistake we made in 2024" | High drama, origin story, relatable vulnerability (Paul Smith framework). |
| **Day 3** | T+48h (02:00 PM) | "How Acme Corp replaced 6 manual tools with 1 swarm" | Epiphany bridge, case study proof, introducing the unique mechanism. |
| **Day 4** | T+72h (10:00 AM) | "The hidden cost of manual client coordination" | Hidden benefits, urgency agitation, addressing top 3 objections. |
| **Day 5** | T+96h (06:00 PM) | "Closing this cohort tomorrow (Last call)" | Grand Slam offer presentation, urgency deadline, hard risk reversal. |

## ⚡️ The Sabri Suby 9-Word Re-Engagement Sequence
Sent to leads unresponsive for 30+ days:

> **Subject:** Quick question about {{company_name}}  
> **Body:** Are you still looking to automate your backend operations this quarter?

---

## 🛡 Anti-Spam & Deliverability Protocol
* Enforces SMTP bridge pacing: Max 500 emails/day, max 200/hour, 20-second spacing between transmissions.
* Zero spam trigger words in subject lines ("FREE", "$$$", "GUARANTEED").
* One-click unsubscribe and physical company address in every footer.
