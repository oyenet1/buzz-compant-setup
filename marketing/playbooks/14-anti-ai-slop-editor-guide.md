---

## 🚫 The Banned AI Slop Lexicon

The following words and phrases are strictly banned from all client-facing copy, proposals, ads, and social content:

| Banned AI Cliché | Why It Fails | What to Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| *"In today's fast-paced digital world..."* | Generic, lazy opener; zero tension. | Start directly with the raw pain or metric. |
| *"Delve", "Tapestry", "Realm"* | Obvious LLM signature words. | Use simple, concrete words: "look into", "network", "domain". |
| *"Game-changer", "Revolutionary"* | Unsubstantiated hype. | Show the hard metric (e.g. "cuts latency by 70%"). |
| *"Elevate your business to new heights"* | Meaningless corporate vaporware. | "Increase monthly net revenue by ₦3,000,000". |
| *"Furthermore", "Moreover", "It is crucial to note"* | Robotic academic transitions. | Use natural spoken transitions: "And here's why", "The catch:", "So". |
| *"A testament to our commitment"* | Bureaucratic filler. | "We back this with a 100% money-back guarantee." |

---

## ✍️ The 4 Rules of Human Editorial Polish
1. **Vary Sentence Length**: Combine 4-word punchy sentences with longer descriptive lines to create natural musical cadence.
2. **Active Verbs Over Passive State**: Write "Our swarm deploys servers" instead of "Servers are deployed by the swarm".
3. **Concrete Nouns Over Abstract Ideas**: Write "PostgreSQL database" instead of "scalable data architecture solution".
4. **Verbatim Client Phrasing**: Use the exact words real business owners use when complaining about their problems.
